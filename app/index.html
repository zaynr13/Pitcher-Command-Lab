TRANSPARENCY IS PART OF THE PRODUCT

What the model knows. What it doesn’t.

Command Lab predicts outcomes from historical observations. It does not establish that changing a target causes a better result.

01. Estimated execution

Pitcher/pitch-type Gaussian errors are partially pooled toward pitch-type and league priors. Targets use the estimated pre-pitch glove position. Published in-sample inferred targets are excluded.

02. Hitter response

Regularized probability models estimate swing, called strike, and contact outcomes. Location interactions let hitters and pitch types differ. Models with and without player effects compete on July validation.

03. Honest evaluation

Training ends June 30, July selects models, and August onward is held out. Modeled run value uses a cross-fitted linear feature and boosted trees. It is a prediction, not a causal estimate or xwOBA.

04. Limits that matter

Glove position is not verified intent. Camera reconstruction introduces correlated measurement error. Bunting, hit-by-pitch, automatic balls, pitch sequencing, injuries and changing arsenals are not simulated. The game ends at the plate appearance.
